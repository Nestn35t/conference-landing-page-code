Why AIBP C&E?

Each AIBP Conference & Exhibition is a private, closed-door event designed for local enterprises and government agencies to access innovative B2B technology solutions, assess where they are on their digital transformation journey, and advocate local digital transformation initiatives.

  • Curated sessions with local government & enterprise stakeholders
  • 40+ B2B technology solutions showcased per event
  • Private roundtables, workshops & executive luncheons
  • Endorsed by local ministries & cybersecurity agencies
