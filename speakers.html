Why Speak at AIBP

Exclusive benefits when you join our platform of ministers, government leaders, and industry pioneers across ASEAN

🏛

Speak Alongside Ministers & Government Leaders

Join a platform where ministers, deputy ministers, and senior government officials from across ASEAN share the stage. Your presence alongside these leaders increases your credibility and positions you as a thought leader in the region.

🎯

Address a Select Group of Decision Makers

Present to an exclusive, invitation-only audience of 600+ C-suite leaders, government officials, and enterprise heads. 75% of attendees are senior decision makers from local enterprises and government agencies — not a general public audience.

🌏

Speak Across ASEAN & Multiple Countries

Distinguished speakers are invited to present across multiple AIBP editions in Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, and the Philippines — expanding your influence across the entire region, not just one country.

🏖

Exclusive Innovation Retreat

Join fellow speakers for a private retreat experience — network with advisory board members, ministers, and C-suite peers in an intimate setting away from the main conference.

🎙

Feature on the AIBP ASEAN Podcast

Selected speakers are invited to feature on the AIBP ASEAN Podcast, reaching thousands of enterprise leaders and government officials across the region. Amplify your thought leadership beyond the stage.

✈

Complimentary Speaker Package

Selected speakers receive complimentary airfare, hotel accommodation, 2 delegate passes, and exclusive access to the Innovation Retreat and private networking events.
