Speaking Formats

Multiple ways to share your expertise

Keynote Presentation

20 – 30 minutes

Main stage presentation to the full conference audience of 600+ attendees. Past keynote speakers include CEOs from PETRONAS, Maybank, and the National AI Office of Malaysia. Ideal for sharing a bold vision, national strategy, or enterprise transformation story that sets the tone for the conference.

Panel Discussion

45 – 60 minutes

Moderated dialogue with 4–5 panelists from enterprise CxOs, government leaders, and industry experts. Each conference features 6–7 thematic sessions covering AI, cybersecurity, data governance, ESG, and talent. Panelists share real experiences — what worked, what broke, and what they would do differently.

Private Workshop

2 – 3 hours

Hands-on, invite-only sessions for a select group of senior attendees. Participants work through frameworks, assess their own readiness, and leave with actionable takeaways. Past workshops have covered data governance, cyber resilience for critical infrastructure, and AI implementation playbooks.

Private Networking

1 – 2 hours

Hosted luncheon or roundtable with a curated guest list of 15–30 C-suite executives. Combines a short keynote or fireside chat with open discussion over a seated meal. Designed for deeper relationship-building and focused dialogue on a single strategic topic with decision makers.
