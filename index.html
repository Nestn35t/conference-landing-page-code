Frequently Asked Questions

AIBP C&E is a private, closed-door event. Each session is curated for a specific audience who are invited to attend based on inputs by our local stakeholders. Please await your confirmation email before making any travel arrangements.

The event is designed for C-Suite executives, Directors, and Heads of Department from local enterprises and government agencies across Malaysia and ASEAN. Local enterprises and government agencies form more than 70% of attendees.

Complimentary delegate passes are available. The conference is also HRDCorp Claimable for Malaysian companies incorporated in Malaysia.

Held annually since 2013, AIBP C&E Malaysia is designed for local enterprises and government agencies to ACCESS innovative B2B technology solutions, ASSESS where they are on their digital transformation journey, and ADVOCATE local digital transformation initiatives.

Exhibiting company and sponsor numbers are capped to ensure an optimal mix of participants. Contact aibp@industry-platform.com for sponsorship and exhibition packages.

After completing your registration, email aibp@industry-platform.com with your full name and passport details. We will issue the letter within 3 business days.
