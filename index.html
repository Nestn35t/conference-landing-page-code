Why This Event, Why Now

As AI adoption accelerates across Southeast Asia, business leaders face unprecedented decisions on strategy, governance, and workforce transformation. AIBP Summit brings together the region's most influential executives to share proven frameworks and forge partnerships that drive measurable outcomes.

Who Attends

C-Suite executives, VPs, Directors, and Heads of Department from Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and high-growth enterprises across ASEAN. Industries include financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, technology, and telecommunications.
