Visa Information

Malaysia offers visa-free entry or visa-on-arrival for most ASEAN nationalities. For delegates from other countries, we can provide an invitation letter to support your visa application. Contact us at aibp@industry-platform.com

Frequently Asked Questions

This is a closed-door, curated event. Registration is required and attendees must receive a confirmation email before making any travel arrangements.

The event is designed for C-Suite executives, Directors, and Heads of Department from enterprises, government agencies, and technology companies across ASEAN. Industries include BFSI, energy, telecoms, manufacturing, and public sector.

Business attire is recommended for all sessions and networking events.

Select sessions may be recorded. Registered attendees will be notified about recording availability after the event.

After completing your registration, email aibp@industry-platform.com with your full name and passport details. We will issue the letter within 3 business days.

Exhibiting company and sponsor numbers are capped to ensure an optimal mix of participants. Contact aibp@industry-platform.com for sponsorship and exhibition packages.
